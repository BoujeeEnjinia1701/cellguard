# BOM notes

- Line numbers 1 to 15, 17, 18 and 19 match the callouts in `media/exploded.png` and the parts in `cad/src/model.py`; for line 15 only the status light is drawn. Line 16 (power cables) is not shown in the model. Lines 17 and 18 were changed and added on 2026-09-30 to make the design buildable (CGD-DDR-003); lines 4, 13 and 15 were respecified and line 19 added on 2026-10-02 to carry Amish's decisions of that day (CGD-DEC-001).
- Every line is priced. Prices are indicative single-unit prices in USD (September and October 2026 estimates) and will change. They exclude cells, tools, shipping and taxes. The basis for each price changed on 2026-10-02 is in the line's notes.
- Total: $159.15 (checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`, CGD-CAL-001 v0.6 section 11). That is $124.00 as at TRL 2, $10.00 for the secondary protector and SCP fuse (line 14), $2.00 more on line 17 (gap pad, spacers, pillars, screws), $2.00 for the copper fuse link (line 18), then $20.00 more for the 1.5 mΩ-class switches (line 4), $0.35 for the status light (line 15) and $0.80 for the light pipe (line 19).
- Value-engineering target: USD 140.00 (`budget_usd: 140` in `project.yaml`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit; raised from $120 by Amish on 2026-09-25, CGD-DDR-002). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 159.15 (USD 19.15 over the target). Against the former $120 target it would be 32.6 % over.
- Lines 3, 6 and 14 follow the choices decided by Amish on 2026-09-25 (CGD-DDR-001, CGD-DDR-002).
- Line 4: 1.5 mΩ-class, 100 V, top-side cooled (TOLT) switches, decided by Amish on 2026-10-02 (CGD-DEC-001) for R6; they bring the board loss at 40 A to 3.47 W (CGD-CAL-001 v0.6). Repriced from $2.50 to $5.00 each: such parts (for example the Infineon IPTC015N10NM5 class, listed by RS Components) are about $4 to $6 each at quantity 10.
- Line 13: printed flame-retardant polycarbonate, opaque for the prototype, with a 3.2 mm hole in the top for the light pipe (CGD-DEC-001, 2026-10-02); sheet aluminium is not used.
- Line 14: an SCP fuse rated for 40 A continuous at 60 V DC or more is not yet confirmed; the price is an estimate.
- Line 15: now includes one three-colour status light (a PLCC-4 RGB LED with three series resistors), $0.35 added (CGD-DEC-001, 2026-10-02).
- Line 19: a round flanged light pipe, 3 mm, pressed into the cover top over the status light, $0.80 (CGD-DEC-001, 2026-10-02).
